# Neden — Language Learning Media Player

**Vision:** A desktop media player for macOS, Windows, and Linux that helps people learn languages through films and shows. Watching, understanding, practising, and saving useful language happen in one continuous experience.

## Core features

- **Full media playback:** Open local videos with fullscreen, keyboard shortcuts, playback speed controls, and subtitle synchronization.
- **Subtitle discovery and display:** Automatically find and download matching OpenSubtitles tracks or load your own. Show source and translation subtitles together, customize their appearance and order, and remember selections per video.
- **Instant word lookup:** Click a word for its meaning. Support translation services and custom dictionary URLs using `%s`, with dictionary pages accessible inside the app.
- **Contextual AI explanations:** Select a word or phrase to pause playback and receive a streamed explanation of its meaning, vocabulary, and grammar using surrounding dialogue. Ask follow-up questions in the same conversation.
- **Fluid viewing:** Replay the selected line, stop an explanation, and close the learning panel to resume watching. Reopen the current conversation without losing your place.
- **Line-by-line practice:** Play one subtitle cue and pause automatically at its end. Move to the previous line, replay the current line, or advance to the next.
- **Flashcards:** Identify useful vocabulary and phrases through subtitle analysis. Review and edit suggested cards, then save them to Anki or built-in spaced repetition.
- **Personal settings and privacy:** Choose learning and explanation languages, AI and lookup services, optional provider fallback, and translation context. Store credentials securely and make external requests and costs clear.

**Success:** Learners can understand unfamiliar dialogue, practise it, and save what matters without breaking their viewing flow.

## Additional feature ideas

Ranked from most focused and useful to least, assuming suitable subtitles are available.

1. **Searchable transcript:** Browse the full dialogue, search for words, and jump directly to a scene.
2. **Rich flashcards:** Include the original sentence, audio clip, optional screenshot, and a link back to the video.
3. **Listening-first mode:** Hide subtitles or translations until requested, revealing only as much help as needed.
4. **Personal difficulty settings:** Adapt explanations and vocabulary suggestions to the learner’s level, goals, and preferred grammatical detail.
5. **Review-aware card suggestions:** Avoid duplicate cards and prioritize useful expressions that recur across videos.
6. **Session review:** Revisit words looked up, bookmarked lines, and difficult passages after watching.
7. **Phrase collection:** Save idioms, sentence patterns, and personal notes alongside individual vocabulary.
8. **Shadowing practice:** Repeat a line, leave time to imitate it, and optionally record yourself for comparison.
9. **Vocabulary memory:** Mark words as known, learning, or unfamiliar; highlight learning words when they appear again.
10. **Cross-video examples:** Find other occurrences of a saved word or expression in the learner’s indexed subtitle library.
11. **Episode preparation:** Preview useful recurring words and expressions before watching, without revealing plot details.
12. **Dictation practice:** Listen and type what you hear, then compare your answer with the subtitles.
13. **Missing-subtitle assistance:** Offer optional transcription or translation when suitable subtitles are unavailable, clearly distinguishing generated text.
